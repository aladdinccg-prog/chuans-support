Common questions

Why is Record Audio off?

New installations start with Microphone input, Record Audio off, and Dark appearance. Record Audio controls the optional saved recording. Turning it off does not stop transcription from processing audio or sending it to a selected cloud engine. Existing saved preferences are preserved.

Can I use Chuans on-device?

Yes, supported Apple speech and downloaded local speech models process recognition on your device. Apple translation uses installed language assets. If you separately enable cloud translation or source review, transcript text can still be sent to that provider. Downloads require a connection.

A microphone, language, or model is not working. What should I check?

Check Chuans permissions in your device’s Settings. Confirm that the selected engine supports your language and its required assets have finished downloading. For cloud engines, check your provider account, API key, endpoint, model access, and network connection. Never send your API key to support.

How do subscriptions and the free trial work?

A seven-day trial of all Chuans functions starts automatically on first launch on each device. No sign-in or purchase is needed to start it. Its start date is stored on that device; it does not restart on ordinary relaunch, renew automatically, or charge you when it ends.

Monthly Subscription is US$9.99 per month in the United States. Prices vary by country or region and Apple shows your local price before purchase. If you choose Subscribe, the paid subscription starts when Apple confirms your purchase, with no additional Apple free trial. It renews automatically unless canceled. A subscription does not include third-party provider API usage.

One subscription works on iPhone and iPad using the same App Store Apple Account. After the local trial ends without an active subscription, iPhone users can continue Apple Live transcription without translation; other engines, translation, Interpretation, Audio and History require a subscription. On iPad, capture and other paid features require a subscription after the trial. The app and Settings remain accessible on both devices.

How do I use an existing subscription or cancel it?

Chuans automatically checks Apple’s verified subscription status. Use the same App Store Apple Account on your iPhone and iPad. For an active subscription, open Chuans Settings and tap Subscription (Manage Subscription) to manage or cancel it in Apple’s sheet. You can also use your device’s Settings: your name, then Subscriptions. Cancellation stops future renewal; access continues through the current paid period. The separate automatic seven-day app trial needs no cancellation. Apple’s cancellation instructions.

Where are my recordings and transcripts?

Chuans keeps history files on your device, and you can export using the system share sheet. Delete individual unwanted files in History. Deleting one item does not necessarily remove a separate recording, exported copy, device backup, or data held by a cloud provider. See the privacy policy for details.
