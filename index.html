Engines, downloads and cloud services

Chuans offers supported Apple features and downloaded on-device models, and lets you configure supported cloud services. Each feature shows engines according to their task capabilities.

  • Cloud request failed: check your endpoint, region, model, API key, account balance and provider permissions. Make sure your provider account settings match the selected endpoint.
  • Cloud permission: adding a key does not authorize content transfer. Allow the selected provider and server address, then retry. Stop active processing before withdrawing permission, or switch to an on-device engine.
  • Charges: providers bill according to their account terms. API usage charges are separate from your Chuans App Store subscription.

Subscriptions, restoring purchases and cancellation

A seven-day trial starts on first use of the app on each device. It does not charge you or renew automatically. A paid subscription requires confirmation in Apple's purchase interface; check the price, period and renewal terms shown there.

Settings shows Subscribe when you do not have a subscription; active subscribers can choose Manage Subscription. One Chuans subscription works on iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same Apple Account used for purchase. Subscription access applies only to the app in which you made the purchase.

  • Purchase not recognized: check that the App Store uses the account used for purchase and that you installed the same app, then choose Restore Purchases in the app's subscription area.
  • iPhone / iPad: choose Manage Subscription in the app, or open Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Mac: choose Manage Subscription in the app, or open your Mac App Store account and manage Subscriptions using the purchasing account.
  • Cannot find a subscription: check your Apple purchase receipt and account. TestFlight test purchases use a separate environment from paid App Store purchases and do not appear as paid subscriptions on normal billing statements.

Apple manages cancellation, renewal and refunds. See Apple's cancellation instructions. Deleting the app or its history does not cancel a subscription.

History, audio and exports

Browse and search saved transcripts and audio in History. Results, optional recordings and session-recovery information are stored on the current device. Subscription access across devices does not mean that history syncs automatically.

  • Use Export or Share where available to save files to your chosen destination. Check the content and recipient before sharing.
  • If audio is missing, check whether recording was enabled for that session. A transcript and a separate recording may not both exist.
  • Deleting one History item may leave separate audio files, exports or backups. Manage those copies separately.

Privacy and contact

On-device processing, cloud processing and saved recordings are separate choices. Cloud transcription sends audio; cloud translation or review sends text. Read the privacy policy for the information categories, recipients, processing locations, permission and deletion details.
