Privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026 · Applies to Chuans on iPhone, iPad and Mac

This policy applies to Chuans and this support website. It explains the purposes, methods and scope of our processing of personal information, and your choices. For privacy questions, contact aladdin.ccg@gmail.com.

Where information is processed depends on the features you choose. On-device speech recognition can be combined with cloud translation or source review. Turning off Record Audio only disables the optional saved recording; it does not stop the selected engine from processing or receiving audio.

1. Audio, transcripts and translation

When you start the relevant features, Chuans processes microphone audio, audio files you select, and audio captured through the system-audio feature. On iPhone and iPad, system audio is obtained through Apple ReplayKit broadcasting; the app does not process its video frames. On Mac, system audio is obtained through Apple Core Audio without capturing screen images. Enabling Include Microphone also processes microphone audio. Transcripts, translations and related session information are used to display and save your results.

On-device features: supported Apple speech recognition, downloaded local speech models and Apple translation language assets perform their respective processing on the device.

Cloud features: choosing cloud transcription sends audio to your configured service. Cloud translation or source review sends transcript text and processing instructions.

If you configure a custom or proxy endpoint, its operator may also receive requests. Depending on the provider and task, processing may involve temporarily uploading files to a remote server. Before sending sensitive content, review the provider's policies, retention controls and processing locations.

Cloud-processing permission: before using a configured cloud provider, Chuans asks for permission to send selected audio or transcript text to that provider and server address. An existing API key does not constitute permission. Choosing Not Now prevents the blocked action from sending content. After allowing it, retry the action yourself; the app does not restart it automatically. You can change permission in the provider's settings after stopping its active cloud processing.

Chuans does not route transcription or translation requests through a developer-operated processing server. The app contains no advertising, cross-app tracking or service that automatically uploads analytics to the developer.

2. System permissions and what happens if you decline

  • Microphone: used for microphone transcription, interpretation or additional microphone recording that you start. Declining prevents the corresponding microphone features from working.
  • Speech recognition: used for supported Apple speech-recognition features; the system may ask for separate permission.
  • System audio: iPhone and iPad require you to start a screen broadcast; Mac requires system-audio recording permission. Declining or stopping it stops the corresponding audio capture.
  • File access: used to read files you select through the system file picker, or save exports to the location you choose.

The system requests permissions when the relevant feature needs them. You can change permissions in Settings on iPhone or iPad, or System Settings → Privacy & Security on Mac. Declining one permission does not affect features that do not need it. The app does not request contacts, location or advertising-tracking permission.

3. Information stored on your device

  • History: transcripts, optional recordings, structured result files and session-recovery information are stored in the app's local documents.
  • Credentials: provider API keys and other secret fields are stored in Apple Keychain. Nonsecret settings, selected models and endpoints are stored in app preferences.
  • Temporary audio: the iPhone and iPad system-audio feature uses temporary audio packets shared by the app and broadcast extension. Acknowledged packets are removed. Mac system-audio packets pass through memory; if you enable recording, the recording is saved separately as a local file.
  • Exports and backups: exported files go to the destination you choose. Device backups may include app files, depending on your Apple or device settings.

Removing a provider from one task's list does not necessarily erase its shared account credentials, because other parts of the app may still use them.

4. Retention, deletion and your choices

You choose the input, processing engine, provider and optional recording settings. You can stop a session, revoke device permissions, avoid cloud features and delete unwanted files from History. Deleting a History item removes the selected file and its associated structured text-row file, but may leave separately saved audio, transcripts, exports or backups. Delete those copies separately where they are stored.

After processing, the app attempts to remove certain temporary files uploaded to providers, but remote cleanup is not guaranteed. For data retained by a cloud provider, contact that provider or use its account controls. Uninstalling Chuans should not be treated as revoking provider API credentials; revoke keys with the provider when needed.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to handle requests and maintain necessary support records, subject to applicable requirements. You can request deletion of correspondence or ask what support information we hold. Using the email address below, you can request access to, a copy of, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, withdraw optional processing permission or ask us to explain this policy. We will handle requests after verifying necessary information. If a request cannot be met, we will explain why and any available alternatives. Do not send identity documents without being asked. Information held only on your device or by an independent provider must be handled on that device or through that provider; we can provide guidance. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before it.

Chuans does not require a separate developer-hosted user account. Ending a subscription does not itself delete recordings, transcripts, settings or provider credentials.

5. Responsible use

Audio and transcripts may contain information about other people. Obtain necessary permission before recording or sharing conversations. Consider the sensitivity of the content before choosing a cloud provider or sharing an export. Audio or text may contain sensitive personal information; provide only what the selected task needs and obtain the required consent of the people concerned. Minors should use the app under a guardian's guidance. Users under fourteen should obtain guardian consent before using features that process personal information and must follow the selected provider's age requirements.

6. Questions and policy updates

For support or privacy questions, contact aladdin.ccg@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
